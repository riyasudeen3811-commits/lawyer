LEGAL INSIGHTS

Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Why is a 30-year title search essential?

A 30-year search examines chain of title, inheritance, encumbrances, and court claims to ensure legal and marketable title.

❓ Do daughters have equal rights in ancestral property in Tamil Nadu?

Yes, daughters are legal coparceners with equal inheritance rights by birth under the Hindu Succession Act.
